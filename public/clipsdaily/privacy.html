Clipsdaily — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Clipsdaily ("the Service") is a personal automation project operated by Abel Prasad. It watches the operator's own live streams on Twitch and Kick, cuts short highlight clips from them, and posts those clips to the operator's own TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube accounts.

What data the Service handles

  • Connected social accounts. To post clips, the Service stores authorization tokens (OAuth) for the operator's own TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube accounts. These tokens live on the operator's own server and are used only to publish the operator's own clips.
  • Video files. The Service records the operator's own live streams, cuts highlight clips from those recordings, and uploads the clips to the operator's own social accounts.
  • Stream chat data. The Service reads public chat messages on the operator's own streams only to detect highlight moments (for example, spikes in chat activity). Chat messages are not stored long-term, shared, or sold.

What the Service does not collect

  • It does not collect personal data from viewers, visitors, or any third party.
  • This website does not use cookies, analytics, or tracking.
  • It does not collect names, email addresses, or payment information from anyone other than the operator.

Sharing and selling

The Service does not share, rent, or sell any data to anyone. Video clips are published only to the operator's own social media accounts.

Data retention and control

Authorization tokens can be revoked at any time by disconnecting the connected account on the relevant platform (TikTok, Instagram, or YouTube), which immediately stops the Service from posting to that account. Recorded streams and clips are stored on the operator's own server and deleted under the operator's own storage policy.

Children's privacy

The Service is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent change.
