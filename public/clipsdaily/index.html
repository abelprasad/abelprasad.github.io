clipsdaily.

Clipsdaily is a personal automation project. It watches the owner's own live streams on Twitch and Kick, cuts short highlight clips from them, and posts those clips to the owner's own TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube accounts.

It posts only content the owner created or has the right to post, and only to accounts the owner controls.
